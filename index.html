About us

Cookit.de started with a simple idea by two friends who loved cooking but were tired of the chaos of scattered notes, cookbooks, and smartphone screenshots. We wanted a central place to save, organize, and effortlessly share our favorite meals. Now, we are building a digital recipe collection for everyone, making cooking simpler, cleaner, and more fun.
